Humanized Antibody Activity Restoring Service

We restored the humanized antibody activity in a failed humanization project conducted by another company. Restoring the activity of a humanized antibody is not simply reverting to the amino acids of the murine antibody. It requires individual analysis for each specific case. We successfully restored the antibody activity and received positive feedback from our customer.
